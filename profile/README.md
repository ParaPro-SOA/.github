# ParaPro

ParaPro is an Australian financial advice workspace for preparing Statements of Advice (SOAs). It brings client facts, adviser decisions, calculations, supporting evidence and document review into one case file.

## From client file to issued advice

An advice team records and confirms the client's circumstances, defines the scope of advice, and reviews the strategy and its calculations. ParaPro assembles an SOA draft from that work, shows what still needs attention, and keeps the review and approval steps connected to the exact document issued.

The adviser remains responsible for the advice. ParaPro is designed to help advisers, paraplanners and their colleagues prepare a document they can examine, correct and approve. It can work alongside an existing practice platform rather than requiring a practice to replace its client system.

## Current stage

The application is a proof of concept under active development. Its demo uses fictional firms and clients. It is not approved for use to provide real client advice.
